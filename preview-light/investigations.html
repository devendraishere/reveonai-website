Asked in every serious conversation

Straight answers on Investigations

Does the AI decide the root cause?

No. The AI drafts root-cause reasoning from the evidence you provide and flags where the argument is weak — but the root-cause determination, the CAPA, and the closure decision belong to your qualified reviewer. That's by design: it's your investigation, accelerated.

Can it hallucinate a wrong investigation?

No output is released by the AI alone — a qualified human reviews, edits, and signs every result. A proprietary data-minimization technique constrains the model to your provided evidence rather than open-ended generation, and every claim is traceable back to its source, which makes review fast and defensible.

Does it replace our QMS deviation module?

No — it works alongside your existing QMS. The deviation lives in your system of record; Investigations accelerates the thinking and writing in between, and the reviewed output flows back into your QMS. Integration scope is agreed per deployment.

How do we start?

The most common start is a focused, time-boxed pilot on your own recent deviations — so you measure drafting time, consistency, and review effort on your own data before any broader commitment. Talk to us and we'll propose a pilot.
