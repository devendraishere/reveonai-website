What good looks like

Review by exception, done right

"Review by exception" only builds trust if it’s done with discipline. These are the conditions that make it defensible.

  • A complete read, not sampling. Exception review starts with everything read — the shortlist is a product of full coverage, not shortcuts.
  • Flags traceable to the record. Every exception points to its exact location and context, so review is fast and verifiable.
  • Your procedures define human coverage. What reviewers must always check is written down and followed — the AI narrows attention, your SOP defines it.
  • Decisions captured as you go. Each reviewer call logged and attributable — a clean release story after the fact.
  • Validation on your own records. Confidence comes from comparing AI flags against your own review of the same records, before you rely on it.
Common questions

Batch-release questions we hear most

What is review by exception in batch record review?

Instead of a person reading every page to find the entries that need judgment, the full record is read by AI and the exceptions are surfaced for human decision. Reviewers spend their time on judgment; coverage comes from the machine’s complete read.

Does the AI release the batch?

Never. Disposition is always a qualified human’s decision. The Batch Records module prepares the review and captures the decisions your people make.

How does this improve right-first-time?

Faster, fresher review catches issues earlier and feeds cleaner data back to the floor — and reviewers who aren’t buried in pages have the attention that right-first-time actually requires.
